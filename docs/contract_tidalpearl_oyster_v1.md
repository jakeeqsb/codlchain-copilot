# TidalPearl Oysters Live Oyster Transport Contract

This contract sets the conditions under which ColdLogix ("the Carrier") transports live oysters for TidalPearl Oysters ("the Shipper"). Fictional document for demonstration only.

## Article 4: Transport Temperature

The Carrier shall keep the internal container temperature between 1°C and 5°C inclusive.

## Article 5: Humidity

Live oysters dry out in low humidity. The Carrier shall keep relative humidity at 90% or higher. If it stays below 90% for 30 consecutive minutes or more, the Carrier shall notify the Shipper's Quality Assurance team within 2 hours of the start of the event.

## Article 6: Notification of Temperature Excursion

If the internal temperature exceeds 5°C for 20 consecutive minutes or more, the Carrier shall notify the Shipper's Quality Assurance team within 1 hour of the start of the excursion.
