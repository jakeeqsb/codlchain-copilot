# Cold-Chain Temperature Excursion Response SOP (QA-SOP-012)

The standard procedure ColdLogix operations follow when a reefer reports an abnormal condition. Fictional document for demonstration only.

## Section 1: Classify the Cause

When a temperature excursion is detected, check compressor power and door status over the same period. Power near zero points to a compressor stop (power loss or compressor failure). A door-open record points to the door being left open during loading, unloading, or inspection. If both are normal and only the temperature jumps, suspect the sensor and cross-check it against a nearby sensor.

## Section 2: Compressor Stop

Contact the driver immediately to confirm whether the compressor can be restarted. If it is not restored within 15 minutes, transfer the cargo to a backup reefer at the nearest depot. After the trip, the maintenance team shall inspect the compressor and power system before the reefer is reassigned.

## Section 3: Door Left Open

Instruct the crew to close the door and resume work only after the temperature recovers. A door left open for more than 10 consecutive minutes is recorded as an unloading-procedure violation, and the crew receives refresher training.

## Section 4: Incident Report

Every temperature excursion shall be reported to the Quality team within 24 hours of its start. The report states the reefer, cargo, excursion period, peak temperature, cause, and actions taken. Where a shipper contract sets its own notification duty, the contract takes precedence.
