# OceanPeak Tuna Trading Ultra-Low-Temperature Tuna Transport Contract

This contract sets the conditions under which ColdLogix ("the Carrier") transports ultra-low-temperature frozen tuna for OceanPeak Tuna Trading ("the Shipper"). Fictional document for demonstration only.

## Article 4: Transport Temperature

The Carrier shall keep the internal container temperature between −60°C and −50°C inclusive. Above −50°C, the flesh of the tuna may discolor.

## Article 5: Immediate Notification

If the internal temperature exceeds −50°C, regardless of duration, the Carrier shall notify the Shipper's Logistics team within 30 minutes of the start of the excursion.

## Article 6: Flesh-Color Inspection

If the internal temperature exceeds −50°C for 10 cumulative minutes or more during the shipment, the cargo shall be inspected for flesh color by an independent inspection body. Where the cause lies with the Carrier, the Carrier bears the inspection cost.

## Article 7: Unloading Door Limit

During unloading, the container door shall not stay open for more than 10 consecutive minutes. If it does, the Carrier shall report the event to the Shipper's Logistics team within 24 hours, and a penalty of 30% of the freight charge applies if a temperature excursion followed.
