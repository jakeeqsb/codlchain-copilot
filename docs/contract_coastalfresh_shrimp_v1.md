# CoastalFresh Seafood Frozen Shrimp Transport Contract

This contract sets the conditions under which ColdLogix ("the Carrier") transports frozen shrimp for CoastalFresh Seafood ("the Shipper"). Fictional document for demonstration only.

## Article 4: Transport Temperature

The Carrier shall keep the internal container temperature between −25°C and −18°C inclusive. Shrimp that rises above −18°C shall not be refrozen, and the Shipper may reject the full lot.

## Article 5: Early Warning of Equipment Abnormality

If the reefer assigned to a shipment shows signs of failure, including compressor power consumption 20% or more above its own recent baseline, the Carrier shall report it to the Shipper's Logistics team within 24 hours and submit a plan for backup equipment, whether or not a temperature excursion has occurred.

## Article 6: Equipment Replacement

After a report under Article 5, the Shipper may require that a different reefer be used from the next shipment onward, and the Carrier shall comply.
