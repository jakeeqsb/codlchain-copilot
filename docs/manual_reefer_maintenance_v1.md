# Reefer Preventive Maintenance Manual (MNT-007)

How ColdLogix judges signs of reefer compressor failure and what maintenance follows. Fictional document for demonstration only.

## Section 1: Power Baseline

Each reefer's baseline is its own average compressor power over the preceding 48 hours. If power rises above the baseline while the temperature stays normal, the compressor is working harder to hold the same temperature.

## Section 2: Power 20% or More Above Baseline

If average power over the last 24 hours is 20% or more above the baseline, check for refrigerant leaks, a dirty condenser coil, and damaged door gaskets, and schedule maintenance within 48 hours.

## Section 3: Power 40% or More Above Baseline

If average power is 40% or more above the baseline, treat the reefer as close to failure: stop assigning it to new trips and send it for maintenance immediately.

## Section 4: Compressor Stop History

A reefer whose compressor stopped during a trip, for any reason, shall have its compressor and power system inspected before it is reassigned.
