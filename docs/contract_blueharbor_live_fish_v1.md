# BlueHarbor Fisheries Live Fish Transport Contract

This contract sets the conditions under which ColdLogix ("the Carrier") transports live fish for BlueHarbor Fisheries ("the Shipper"). Fictional document for demonstration only.

## Article 5: Transport Temperature

The Carrier shall keep the internal container temperature between 0°C and 4°C inclusive from loading to unloading, and shall record it at least once per minute.

## Article 6: Notification of Temperature Excursion

If the internal temperature exceeds 4°C for 15 consecutive minutes or more, the Carrier shall notify the Shipper's Quality Assurance team by phone and email within 1 hour of the start of the excursion. The notice shall state the reefer number, the start and end of the excursion, the peak temperature, and the suspected cause.

## Article 7: Inspection on Arrival

Cargo affected by an excursion that requires notice under Article 6 shall be inspected at the destination in the presence of the Shipper. The Carrier shall not unload or deliver the cargo before the inspection is complete.

## Article 8: Right to Refuse Delivery

If the internal temperature exceeds 4°C for 30 cumulative minutes or more during the shipment, the Shipper may refuse delivery. The Carrier shall hold the cargo until the Shipper decides. Where the cause lies with the Carrier, the Carrier shall refund the freight charge and compensate the actual loss of the cargo.

## Article 9: Temperature Records

On the Shipper's request, the Carrier shall provide the temperature record of the full shipment within 72 hours.
